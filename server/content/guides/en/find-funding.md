There is no single best source of money. The right one depends on how much you need, how fast, and what it is for.

## Your own savings first

The cheapest money is money you already have. Saving a little every week (see *Separate business and personal money*) means you can buy stock in bulk at a better price without borrowing.

## Savings groups and susu

- **Village savings and loans groups (VSLA)** and similar groups: members save together and lend to each other at agreed terms. Good for small amounts and for building the saving habit.
- **Susu collectors:** convenient for daily saving. Use collectors who are registered and known in your area, and keep your own record of every contribution.

## Microfinance and savings and loans companies

- Lend small amounts, often without collateral, sometimes to groups.
- Quick, but interest and fees can be high. Compare the **total amount to repay**.
- Check that the company is licensed by the **Bank of Ghana** before you save or borrow with them.

## Rural and community banks, and commercial banks

- Better rates for larger amounts, but more documents: registration, statements, often collateral.
- A business account with steady deposits over several months improves your chances.

## Government and development programmes

- The **Ghana Enterprises Agency (GEA)** runs programmes for MSMEs, including training, business advice through **Business Advisory Centres**, and from time to time grants or supported loans with partners.
- **Development Bank Ghana** works through partner banks to lend to small businesses.
- Development partners and NGOs sometimes run grant or challenge funds for particular sectors, such as agro-processing or youth and women-owned businesses.

These programmes usually ask for a registered business, records and a short plan. Visit your nearest GEA Business Advisory Centre to ask what is open now.

## Watch out

- No genuine grant asks you to pay a fee to "release" the money.
- Be careful of quick loan apps with very high charges and aggressive collection.
- Never share your MoMo PIN with anyone.
