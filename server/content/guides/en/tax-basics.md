Paying tax is part of running a formal business, and being tax compliant opens doors: government contracts, some loans and support programmes ask for it.

The **Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA)** runs a simpler system for small, informal businesses called the **Modified Taxation Scheme (MTS)**.

> Tax rules change. Treat this guide as a starting point and confirm what applies to you with GRA, at a Taxpayer Service Centre or through your trade association.

## Your tax number

As an individual, your **Ghana Card number** is your Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). Have it ready when you register for anything with GRA.

## The Modified Taxation Scheme

It is for individuals in the informal sector, such as traders, artisans and vendors. GRA groups businesses by their yearly sales:

- **Sales of GHS 20,000 a year or less (on average):** you pay a **fixed amount** set by GRA for your type and size of business.
- **Sales above GHS 20,000 and up to GHS 500,000 a year:** you pay **3% of your total sales**.
- **Others** (for example professionals, people with several businesses or outlets, partners in a registered partnership, or anyone who chooses it) are taxed on **profit**: sales minus allowable business costs.

This is why keeping sales records matters: your records show which group you belong in and how much you owe.

## How to register and pay

- **Register** with your Ghana Card, GhanaPost digital address and phone number, using GRA's **MTS Taxpayer App**, at a GRA office, through a GRA field officer, or through your trade association.
- **Pay** with Mobile Money in the app, or by dialling **\*880#**.
- At the end of the year, report your actual sales in the app.

## VAT: only for bigger businesses

From January 2026, a business that sells goods only has to register for **VAT** when its sales are above **GHS 750,000 a year**. Most micro and small businesses are below this. If you think you are close, speak to GRA.

## Using this app for tax

- Record every sale, and your monthly sales total is on the Home screen.
- Keep receipts for expenses (you can photograph them).
- At year end, add up your monthly sales to know which MTS group you fall in.
