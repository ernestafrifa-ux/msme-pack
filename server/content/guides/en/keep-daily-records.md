Good records are not about accounting. They answer three simple questions:

- How much money do I have right now?
- Did I make a profit this month?
- Where is my money going?

## Record the same day

Memory fades fast. A sale you forget to record today is gone by next week. Pick a fixed moment, such as closing time or before you sleep, and spend five minutes recording.

In this app:

- **Sale**: every time money comes in for goods or services.
- **Expense**: every time money goes out for the business: rent, transport, light bill, data, packaging.
- **Buy stock**: when you buy goods to sell, so your stock count goes up too.
- **Transfer**: when you move money, for example cash deposited into MoMo. It is not a sale or an expense.

## Small amounts matter

Trotro fares, MoMo charges and "small small" purchases often add up to more than people expect. Record them too. The MoMo fee box on a sale or expense helps you see how much the charges cost you each month.

## Check once a week

Once a week, count the money in your cash box and check your MoMo balance. Compare with what the app says on the Home screen. If they are different, look for a sale or expense you forgot.

## Look at the month

At the end of each month, look at **This month** on the Home screen:

- Are sales going up or down?
- Which expense is biggest?
- Is the estimated profit enough to pay you and grow?

These answers help you decide what to sell more of, what to cut, and when to restock.
