Credit can help you keep good customers, but unpaid credit is one of the fastest ways for a small business to run out of money to restock.

## Decide who gets credit

Set simple rules and stick to them:

- only regular customers you know well,
- a **limit** for each one (for example, not more than GHS 500 owed at any time),
- no new credit until the last one is paid.

## Always write it down

A credit sale you only "remember" is easy to argue about later. Use an **invoice** in this app:

- it lists the items, the total and the date to pay by,
- the customer gets a link on WhatsApp showing exactly what they owe,
- products on the invoice come out of your stock when you send it.

## Make paying easy

Put your MoMo number and name in **How to pay you** on the invoice. Customers pay faster when they don't have to ask how.

## Follow up on time

- **A day before** the date to pay: a friendly reminder.
- **On the day** it becomes overdue: the app marks it **Overdue**. Send the **reminder on WhatsApp**; it shows what is still owed.
- **After a week:** call or visit. Agree on a date or on part payments, and record each payment as it comes.

Be polite, but don't stay silent. Customers pay first the people who ask.

## Record part payments

When a customer pays part, use **Record money received** on the invoice. The invoice shows what is still owed, and the money appears in your cash book.

## Watch the total

The Home screen shows how much customers owe you in total. If that number keeps growing, tighten your credit rules before it hurts your stock.
