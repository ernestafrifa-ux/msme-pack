Most of your customers already use WhatsApp every day. Used well, it brings back old customers and new ones through their friends, at almost no cost.

## Step 1: Use WhatsApp Business

Install the free **WhatsApp Business** app (it can run on a second number). Fill in:

- business name and a clear photo or logo,
- what you sell, opening hours and your location or digital address,
- a **catalogue** with photos and prices of your main products.

## Step 2: Save every customer's number

Ask customers: *"Can I save your number to tell you when new stock comes?"* Most will say yes. Use **labels** to group them, for example "Regular", "Wholesale", "Owes money".

## Step 3: Post on your Status

Your WhatsApp Status is free advertising to everyone who has saved your number.

- Post 2 or 3 times a week: new stock, a customer using your product, a short video of how it is made.
- Always show the **price** and how to order.
- Good, bright photos sell. Take them in daylight on a clean background.

## Step 4: Message, don't spam

Use **broadcast lists** to send news to many customers at once. Only people who saved your number receive broadcasts, so ask them to save it.

- Send useful messages: new stock, a festive offer, "back in stock".
- Not more than once or twice a week.

## Step 5: Make ordering easy

- Reply quickly. Set a quick reply for common questions like prices and delivery.
- Confirm orders in writing: items, price, delivery date.
- For bigger or credit orders, send an **invoice from this app**; it opens on the customer's phone.

## Look after regulars

Your regular customers are worth more than new ones. Remember what they buy, thank them, and give them first news of new products.
