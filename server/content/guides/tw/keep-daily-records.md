Akontaa pa nnyɛ akontaabu a ɛyɛ den. Ɛma wunya nsɛmmisa mmiɛnsa yi ho mmuaeɛ:

- Sika ahe na mewɔ seesei?
- Menyaa mfasoɔ bosome yi anaa?
- Me sika kɔ he?

## Kyerɛw no da no ara

Nkaeɛ yera ntɛm. Adetɔn a wo werɛ firi sɛ wobɛkyerɛw no nnɛ no, dapɛn a ɛdi hɔ no, ayera. Paw bere pɔtee bi, te sɛ bere a wototo wo pon mu anaa ansa na woada, na fa simma enum kyerɛw.

Wɔ app yi mu:

- **Adetɔn (Sale)**: bere biara a sika ba wɔ nneɛma anaa adwuma bi ho.
- **Ɛka (Expense)**: bere biara a sika fi adwuma no mu kɔ: dan ka, kaa ka, kanea ka, data, nneɛma a wode kyekyere.
- **Tɔ nneɛma (Buy stock)**: bere a wotɔ nneɛma a wode bɛtɔn, sɛnea ɛbɛma wo stock akontaa akɔ soro.
- **Sika a akɔ baabi foforɔ (Transfer)**: bere a wotu sika fi baabi kɔ baabi, te sɛ sika a wode kɔto MoMo mu. Ɛnyɛ adetɔn anaa ɛka.

## Sika nketewa ho hia

Trotro ka, MoMo charges ne nneɛma nketenkete a wotɔ no ka bom yɛ kɛseɛ sene sɛnea nnipa susuw. Kyerɛw eyinom nso. MoMo fee adaka a ɛwɔ adetɔn anaa ɛka so no boa wo ma wohunu sika a charges no gye wo bosome biara.

## Hwɛ no dapɛn biara

Dapɛn biara, kan sika a ɛwɔ wo sika adaka mu na hwɛ wo MoMo balance. Fa toto nea app no kyerɛ wɔ **Fie (Home)** so no ho. Sɛ ɛnyɛ pɛ a, hwehwɛ adetɔn anaa ɛka bi a wo werɛ fii sɛ wobɛkyerɛw.

## Hwɛ bosome no

Bosome biara awieeɛ, hwɛ **Bosome yi** a ɛwɔ Fie so no:

- Adetɔn rekɔ soro anaa ɛresian?
- Ɛka bɛn na ɛso paa?
- Mfasoɔ a wosusu no bɛso atua wo ka na ama adwuma no anyini anaa?

Saa mmuaeɛ yi bɛboa wo ma woasi gyinaeɛ sɛ deɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔn bi pii, deɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wotwa so, ne bere a ɛsɛ sɛ wotɔ nneɛma foforɔ.
