Nnwumayɛfoɔ pii fa boɔ a ɔfoforɔ a ɔbɛn wɔn bɔ no na wɔde bɔ wɔn deɛ. Nanso wo ka nyɛ pɛ sɛ wɔn deɛ. Sɛ wo boɔ ntumi nka wo ka nyinaa a, wobɛtumi ayɛ adwuma da biara na woahwere sika.

## Anammɔn 1: Hwehwɛ sɛnea adeɛ baako bɔ wo

Ka biribiara a ɛhia ansa na adeɛ baako ayɛ krado sɛ wobɛtɔn no bom.

**Sɛ wotɔ na wotɔn bio a:** boɔ a wotuaeɛ, ne kaa ka ne nneɛma soa ka a ɛfa adeɛ no ho.

**Sɛ wo ara woyɛ a:** nneɛma nyinaa a wode yɛ bere baako (batch), gas anaa gyabidie, nneɛma a wode kyekyere ne label. Fa nneɛma dodoɔ a ɛyɛ no kyekyɛ mu.

**Nhwɛsoɔ (shito):** batch baako gye GHS 180 wɔ mako, ngo, nam ne nnuhwam ho, GHS 30 gas, ne toa 20 a label ka ho a ɛbiara yɛ GHS 3 (GHS 60). Batch no nyinaa yɛ GHS 270 na ɛma toa 20, enti toa baako bɔ **GHS 13.50**.

## Anammɔn 2: Fa adwuma no ka afoforɔ ka ho

Dan ka, kanea, nsuo, fon ne wo ankasa akatua nso firi adetɔn mu. Ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ: ka eyinom bom bosome baako, na fa nneɛma dodoɔ a wotɔn bosome baako mu no kyekyɛ mu.

**Nhwɛsoɔ:** GHS 600 bosome baako, na wotɔn toa 300 bosome baako: ɛbɛyɛ **GHS 2 toa baako biara**. Enti toa baako ka nyinaa bɛyɛ GHS 15.50.

## Anammɔn 3: Fa wo mfasoɔ ka ho

Si gyinaeɛ sɛ mfasoɔ ahe na wopɛ wɔ adeɛ baako biara so. Nnwuma nketewa pii bɔ mmɔden sɛ wɔbɛnya 20% kɔsi 50% wɔ ka nyinaa so, ɛgyina adeɛ no ne akansi so.

**Nhwɛsoɔ:** GHS 15.50 ne ne 40% yɛ bɛyɛ GHS 21.70. Yɛ no **GHS 22**.

## Anammɔn 4: Hwɛ dwam

Fa toto boɔ a adetɔfoɔ bɛtua ho. Sɛ wo boɔ yɛ den sene afoforɔ deɛ paa a:

- hwehwɛ wɔn a wɔtɔn ma wo a wɔn boɔ yɛ mmerɛ, anaa wo ne aguadifoɔ afoforɔ ntɔ pii bom,
- ma nneɛma a ɛsɛe no so ntew,
- anaa fa biribi foforɔ ka ho: kyekyere a ɛyɛ fɛ, delivery, anaa nea ɛso.

Sɛ wo boɔ yɛ mmerɛ sene afoforɔ deɛ paa a, ebia worehwere sika a anka wobɛnya.

## Ma wo boɔ nkɔ so nhyia mmerɛ

Sɛ nneɛma anaa kaa ka kɔ soro a, ɛsɛ sɛ wo boɔ nso kɔ soro. Wɔ app yi mu, sɛ wokyerɛw **Tɔ nneɛma (Buy stock)** a, adeɛ no ka (cost price) sesa, na adeɛ no krataafa kyerɛ wo mfasoɔ a wunya wɔ ɛbiara so.
